# Vanessa Souza Odonto — Design Brief

- **Design read:** A reassuring, contemporary dental practice for families and adults who value clear guidance, prevention, and thoughtful care.
- **Concept spine:** A gentle journey from first contact to confident smile, expressed through enamel-like translucency, close-up material detail, and scroll-led camera movement.
- **Delivery tier:** cinema. The continuous scroll-scrub film is the hero mechanic; surrounding sections use restrained transform reveals.
- **Locked palette:** deep teal `#174B4A`, pale mist `#EAF2F2`, clean white `#FFFFFF`, muted coral `#D97870` as the sole accent. Teal signals clinical confidence, mist keeps the experience airy, and coral adds a warm human note.
- **Locked type:** Satoshi display + Inter Tight body; contemporary, legible, warm without becoming playful.
- **Animation mode:** animated-website.
- **Journey shape:** single-shot. A continuous move around a translucent enamel molar creates a coherent story without scene seams.
- **Journey:** 1) Welcome: clinic atmosphere, headline “Cuidado que começa no seu sorriso.” 2) Prevention: closer approach to the tooth, “Prevenção em cada detalhe.” 3) Personalized care: orbit across enamel, “Um plano pensado para você.” 4) Confidence: macro finish, “Mais confiança para sorrir.”
- **World grammar:** identical clean healthcare editorial look, pale mist environment, soft daylight, center-safe translucent tooth, deep teal shadows, restrained coral accent, slow push/orbit/macro movement, no cuts or on-screen text.
- **Mobile framing:** central subject remains within the middle 60%; responsive image/video crops preserve the molar and leave room for overlaid chapter copy.
- **Delivery budget:** desktop clip ≤32 MiB; mobile clip ≤16 MiB.
- **Section plan:** 1) hero/journey, 2) service editorial split, 3) approach horizontal manifesto, 4) practice image-led panel, 5) contact/location typographic close. Layout families: cinematic full-bleed, asymmetric split, horizontal editorial, image canvas, contact band.
- **Asset plan:** scroll-scrub film and exact posters; clinic-room image; enamel detail; abstract texture; custom icon sheet; wordmark/monogram and favicon; OG card.
- **CTA inventory:** hero “Agendar consulta” as coral compact link; service “Conhecer tratamentos” as route-line text CTA; contact “Falar no WhatsApp” as outlined contact panel; nav “Agendar” as small coral stamp.
